📖 Rules & Club Guide

⛳ HOW TO PLAY (TRADITIONAL GOLF)

  • Driver (1D6+4): Longest drive (5–10 hexes); scatter roll applied. Tee only.
  • Long Iron (1D6+2): Strong approach (3–8 hexes); minor scatter. Tee or fairway.
  • Short Iron (1D6): Precise pitch (1–6 hexes); no scatter. Anywhere on land.
  • Putter (1D6: 1–3): Controlled roll (1–3 hexes) for greens & close approaches.
  • Sand Bunkers: Short iron only; -2 distance disadvantage (min 0).
  • Rough / Deep Rough: -1 penalty to all club distance rolls (min 1).
  • Water & Trees (Out of Bounds): Nearest land drop + 1 penalty stroke.
  • Gimme (+1 stroke): Ball adjacent to hole (1 hex away) can be sunk instantly.
  • Slope Arrows (↑): Slide the ball 1 hex in arrow direction upon landing.

⚡ CRAZY GOLF (MINI-GOLF) MODE

  • Putter Stroke Powers: All clubs are variations of how hard you strike the putter:
    • Tap: d6/2 (1–3 hexes movement, no scatter)
    • Flick: d6 (1–6 hexes movement, no scatter)
    • Hit: d6+2 (3–8 hexes movement, d6/3 hexes scatter)
    • Whack: d6+4 (5–10 hexes movement, d6/2 hexes scatter)
    • Blast: d6+6 (7–12 hexes movement, d6/2 hexes scatter)
  • Bumper Rails: All strokes bank and bounce elastically off bumper rails with remaining distance.
  • Speed Ramps: Boosts the ball forward (+1 to +2 distance) across waterways and tracks.
  • Slopes: All strokes slide 1 hex along slope arrows upon landing.
  • Spinning Windmill: Alternates OPEN & BLOCKED each stroke! Time your roll to slip past the blades.
  • Warp Tubes: Roll into cyan portal (IN) to warp instantly out of exit (OUT) with forward momentum.
  • Loop-de-Loop: Roll into spiral funnel with speed to loop directly toward the cup.
